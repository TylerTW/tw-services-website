About T.W Services

Structural steel fabrication and erection from Liberty Hill, Texas.

From a 3,000 sq ft shop to 24,000

T.W Services started in a small 3,000 square foot shop, cutting material with grinders and torches, with a crew of two.

Twenty years later, we've grown into a 24,000 square foot, state-of-the-art facility built to speed up production on your next project, with full-time fabrication and erection crews and services open to the general public.

Why one contract works

We specialize in single-contract, turnkey structural steel, handling both fabrication and erection in a single scope. That minimizes risk, stops the back-and-forth between fabricators and erectors, helps general contractors close scope gaps, and keeps quality control in one team's hands.

Safety and training

  • Heavy investment in OSHA training
  • At least 20 hours of training certifications for every employee
  • AWS D1.1 structural welding certifications
  • Subpart R steel erection certifications
