Commercial Structural Steel

Services for General Contractors

Fabrication and erection in a single scope, so your steel package has one team responsible from estimate to final bolt.

Structural steel fabrication

From concept to completion, we fabricate steel structures that match your vision and requirements, with quality and attention to detail at every stage.

  • Estimating: our experienced estimators catch issues before you submit your number, tightening up scope gaps.
  • Submittals: our team reviews and works with structural engineers so your project gets optimized connection details that speed up erection.
  • Precision: we hold a 1/16" tolerance in our shop, so your steel is dead-on.
fabricated steel in the shop

Erection services

Need a new steel structure erected? Our crews install and assemble steel components safely and efficiently, led by highly trained foremen and journeyman ironworkers.

  • Heavy investment in OSHA training
  • Every employee has at least 20 hours of training certifications
  • AWS D1.1 structural welding certifications
  • Subpart R steel erection certifications

For commercial and industrial projects, we design and build custom steel structures for the specific needs of your business, improving function, durability and appearance.
